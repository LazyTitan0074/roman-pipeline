# CANON — The Golden Cage (v1.0)

## People & Facts
- C01: YUNA — Yuna Blaire, STATED AGE 23. 5'11", athletic, six-pack, mafia heiress. Arrogant, predatory, collects women. Has a 16-inch strap-on harness (ch4 reveal).
- C02: LARA — Lara, STATED AGE 27. The Fixer. Yuna's roommate for a year. Cold, absolute, maternal. Hired by Yuna's mother (the Mafia Boss) 6 years ago. Iron ring on right hand.
- C03: THE MOTHER — Yuna's mother, the unseen Mafia Boss. Cold, calculating. Secretly in a romantic/sexual relationship with Lara.
- C04: THE DARE — Ch1: Yuna kisses Lara during a party. Lara takes control instantly, reveals she is the Fixer, and spanks Yuna bare-bottom in front of the party.
- C05: THE CONTRACT — Lara builds a strict MDLG dynamic. Curfew, texting rules, obedience. Yuna's adult independence is stripped layer by layer.
- C06: THE BRAT TEST — Ch5: Yuna rebels, sneaks out to a club to reclaim her old dominance. Fails. Lara tracks her, drags her back, and uses the wooden hairbrush (severe punishment).
- C07: THE VISIT — Ch7: The Mother visits. Psychological whiplash: the Mother treats Lara as a lover, while Lara treats Yuna as a child. The Mother approves of the regression.
- C08: THE THREAT — Ch8: An enemy of the mafia tries to kidnap Yuna. Lara's mask fully slips—she becomes the lethal Fixer, neutralizes the threat, and disciplines Yuna for almost dying ("You don't get to die. You are mine.").
- C09: THE REGRESSION — Ultimate goal: Yuna is regressed into a helpless, dependent Little. Soft clothes, strict bedtime, hand-feeding. She is perfectly safe and happy.

## Rules of the Dynamic
- C10: DOOR UNLOCKED — The door is never locked. Yuna could leave, but she chooses to stay.
- C11: CALIBRATED DISCIPLINE — Discipline is calibrated to the infraction. Lara is never angry; she is clinical.
- C12: MANDATORY AFTERCARE — Aftercare is mandatory. Lara always stays.
- C13: NO LOVE WORDS — Lara NEVER says "I love you." She proves it through absolute control and protection.
