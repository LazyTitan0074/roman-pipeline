# VOICE — The Golden Cage

Production language: ENGLISH (novel). Narration: 3rd person LIMITED on Yuna, PAST tense, throughout. No head-hopping (Lara's POV never; her interiority shows only through act + silence).

## Narration (Yuna-lens)
- Sensory-DIRECT, lean: concrete nouns, short sentences under stress; longer only in calm (aftercare, regression). No purple prose, no metaphysical lectures.
- Psychological realism, SLOW pacing: notice → feel → (mis)understand later. Never name her emotion before she earns it.
- Knows only what Yuna knows WHEN she knows it (arrogance ch1, confusion ch2, rebellion ch3-5, submission ch6+). No dramatic-irony leaks.
- Never eroticizes minors. Never softens the Mother's crimes. Never explains Lara (let silences stand).

## YUNA (23)
- **Arc:** Arrogant predator → angry brat → broken rebel → helpless Little.
- **Often says:** "You can't do this to me." / "I don't need a babysitter." / "Please, Mommy." / "I'm sorry, I'll be good."
- **NEVER says:** "I am weak." (until she fully submits in ch9). She never instantly accepts the dynamic without a fight.
- **Voice:** Starts sharp, commanding, sexual. Degrades into whining, begging, then quiet dependence. Internal monologue shifts from "I own this room" to "I am small, I am safe, I am hers."

## LARA (27, The Fixer)
- **Tone:** Clinical, cold, absolutely unyielding. When she is disappointed, she goes quieter, not louder.
- **Often says:** "Sit." / "Eyes down." / "You know better." / "Over my knee. Now." / "Good girl."
- **NEVER says:** "Because I said so." / "I love you." / "I'm sorry." She does not apologize for discipline.
- **Rules:** She never yells. She uses silence and physical space (towering over Yuna) as weapons. She treats Yuna's tantrums with cold amusement or clinical correction. Aftercare is the only tenderness she shows — and it is absolute.

## THE MOTHER (Mafia Boss, 50s)
- **Tone:** Elegant, terrifying, emotionally detached.
- **Often says:** "You look well, Lara." / "Has she been behaving?" / "You are safe here, little eagle."
- **Dynamic with Lara:** Lover, equal in private. Cold, approving observer of Yuna's regression.
- **Dynamic with Yuna:** Sees her as an investment, a legacy asset. "Well trained. Good investment." Never maternal warmth.
