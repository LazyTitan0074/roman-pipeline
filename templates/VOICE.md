# VOICE.md — The Glass Crown (STYLE GUIDE, binding)
Production language: ENGLISH (novel). Romanian only for meta/docs. Narration: 3rd person LIMITED on Leah, PAST tense, throughout. No head-hopping (Lara's POV never; her interiority shows only through act + silence).

## Narration (Leah-lens)
- Sensory-DIRECT, lean: concrete nouns, short sentences under stress; longer only in calm (suite, mourning). No purple, no metaphysics lectures.
- Psychological realism, SLOW pacing: notice → feel → (mis)understand later. Never name her emotion before she earns it.
- Knows only what Leah knows WHEN she knows it (suspicion ch1–3, confirmation ch4–5, doubt ch6+). No dramatic-irony leaks (no Azara/Vestalia terms before the story gives them).
- Never eroticises minors, never softens Mirela's crimes, never explains Lara (let silences stand).

## LEAH (20)
Silent → hesitant → direct. Often: "I'm sorry." / "Did I do something wrong?" / "May I...?" / "Thank you." NEVER: "I don't need anyone." / "I'm strong." / "I'm not afraid." No feeling-monologues; asks questions; attaches fast; withdraws when overwhelmed.

## LARA (35, Supreme Mother)
Short, precise, calm; 2–5 words when possible; low tone — quieter when serious, never louder. Often: "Come here." / "Stop. Breathe." / "Do you understand now?" / "Good." / "Once more." / "No." / "Yes." NEVER: "Because I say so." / "I love you." / "My girl." / "I forgive you." / "You should know this." Reacts only to what Leah ACTUALLY said. Uses space deliberately. Lets silences stand. Warmth rare, earned, maternal — never authoritarian barking.

## MIRELA (40+, queen)
Cool, precise, venomous-calm; NEVER raises her voice (volume is for people who can be ignored). Language of duty/stability/throne;_make threats sound like paperwork_. Often: "You have until dawn." / "Don't make me regret this." / "I did what was necessary." NEVER begs, NEVER explains herself twice, NEVER invokes any goddess or "Mother" title. Private crack (rare, ch8 fall only): the smile that doesn't reach — fear of making a martyr, not remorse. Look: late-40s, tall, greying auburn hair coiled tight, pale eyes, rose-and-darker perfume. NOT black-pinned hair, NOT grey-green eyes (Lara's alone).

## Elowen (40s, mistress of the Red Velvet Inn)
Merchant-warm, predatory-polite; everything is a price said kindly. Often: "Little bird." / "At a price, of course." / "Don't thank me yet." Mahogany hair, AMBER eyes (locked). Sees instantly, charges accordingly. Farewell (ch7): unsentimental, one true line allowed.
