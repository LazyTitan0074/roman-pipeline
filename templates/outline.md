# Book Summary
Leah Estia, 20, the legitimate heiress of Valdris, flees a forced marriage to Lord Varnholt and reaches the Red Velvet Inn. There Madam Lara, 35, Queen of the Vestalian Empire, offers her the throne for vassalage. Between rescue and rule, Leah must decide whether Lara's discipline is care or better-packaged abuse — and Lara must decide whether to keep her own limit: you don't correct someone who cannot leave.

# Chapter Details

## Chapter 1: Cinderella
### Summary
After the king's death on the front, Leah (20), daughter of the first marriage, lives as a Cinderella: moved out of the royal apartment, made to serve, humiliated daily by her stepmother Mirela and her stepbrother Cassian (14). Mirela — the former lady-in-waiting who poisoned the first queen and paid mercenaries to withdraw the king's guard — rules alone and wants Leah gone without a martyrdom: a marriage to Lord Varnholt (62) of the north. On the night before the signing, Leah flees to the Lower City.

### Key Events
- Leah serves and is humiliated by Cassian and Mirela — the Cinderella status.
- Mirela presents the betrothal contract with Lord Varnholt — exile disguised.
- Memory of her dead mother and suspicion of poison — the decision to flee.
- Flight through castle and city — guards, fear, hunger.

## Chapter 2: The Lower City
### Summary
Flight, fear, hunger. Leah reaches the Red Velvet Inn, the costliest brothel in the capital, where information is worth more than flesh. Its mistress Elowen recognizes her in the first second — a princess cannot be mistaken — and takes her in out of interest: a virgin princess is a fortune. Leah accepts: for the first time, her body is her own.

### Key Events
- Arrival at the inn and Elowen's recognition.
- The contract: young clients only; information as currency.
- The first night — fear and first autonomy.
- Rumour of a huge army in the east.

## Chapter 3: Two Days

### Key Events

## Chapter 5: The Offer
### Summary

She shows the evidence — Queen Elara's poisoning, the mercenaries' payment. Mira and Vela, two women of the proposed future council, attend as living proof. Leah asks for time; Lara grants it, but the army crosses the border anyway.

### Key Events
